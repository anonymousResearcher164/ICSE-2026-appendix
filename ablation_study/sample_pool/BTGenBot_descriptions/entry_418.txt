The behavior tree is designed for a robot to navigate through an environment with periodic global path replanning at 1 Hz and includes recovery actions. The robot will attempt to navigate to a specified goal, utilizing a grid-based planner for computing the path. If the navigation encounters issues, it will clear the costmaps and retry the navigation a set number of times before resorting to recovery actions. The recovery actions include clearing both local and global costmaps, spinning to reorient itself, and waiting for a specific duration. Additionally, the recovery process is triggered by the "GoalUpdated" event. This behavior tree ensures that the robot can adapt to changing environments and recover from navigation failures during its operation.
