The behavior tree represents a robot's reactive response to obstacles. It consists of a sequence of actions triggered by specific conditions. Initially, it checks the y-coordinate threshold and sets a goal accordingly. If the condition is not met, it then checks the z-coordinate threshold and sets another goal. If neither condition is met, it sets a default goal. This tree allows the robot to dynamically respond to its environment based on the detected obstacles' positions in the y and z coordinates, ensuring safe navigation.
