The mission describes a repeated sequence of logical-state checking, movement, and logical-state updating for a
robot.

First, the robot will execute the sequence num_cycles times.

Next, it will execute the custom action LTLPreCheck, the custom action MoveAction, and the custom action UpdateLTL
in order. If any action fails, the sequence will stop.

Parameters: The custom action LTLPreCheck will read ltl_state_current and ltl_state_desired_sequence from the
blackboard. The custom action MoveAction will store action on the blackboard and read move_base_finished,
move_base_idle, and nav_goal from the blackboard. The custom action UpdateLTL will read and update action_sequence
and ltl_state_desired_sequence on the blackboard and store nav_goal on the blackboard.
