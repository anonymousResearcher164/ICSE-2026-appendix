The mission describes looking at a tray, detecting its handles, picking up the tray, moving to a table, and
placing the tray.

First, the robot will execute the sequence one time.

Next, it will execute the actions in order and stop the sequence if any action fails.

Then, it will execute custom action LookAtTheTray.

After that, it will keep trying to execute custom action DetectHandles until it succeeds or three attempts have
been made.

Next, it will execute custom action DetectHandles with a timeout of 300 milliseconds for each attempt. If an
attempt does not execute within 300 milliseconds, it will interrupt that attempt and stop it.

Then, it will execute custom action PickTray, custom action LookUp, custom action MoveBack, custom action
GoToTable, and custom action PlaceTray in order.
