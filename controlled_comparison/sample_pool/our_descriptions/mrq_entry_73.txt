The mission describes transporting a tray to a table.

First, the robot will execute the tray-transport sequence one time.

Next, it will execute the following actions in order and stop the sequence if any action fails: look at the tray,
detect the handles, pick the tray, look up, move back, go to the table, and place the tray.

Then, it will keep trying to detect the handles until detection succeeds or three attempts are completed.

Finally, it will interrupt each handle-detection attempt if it does not execute within 300 milliseconds.
