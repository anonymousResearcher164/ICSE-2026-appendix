The mission describes a competition routine in which the robot repeatedly moves tomato cans between a table, a
back tray, and a shelf while updating its posture, sensors, and planning scene.

First, the root Sequence will execute FullCompetitionMode in order and will stop if that subtree fails.

Next, the Repeat decorator in FullCompetitionMode will execute its sequence forever. The sequence will first
execute GetInRestPose and then execute DoYourTrick.

Then, the GetInRestPose Sequence will obtain the arm joints for the REST pose and will execute three actions in
parallel. The parallel node will control the head and torso together, set the left arm joints, and set the right
arm joints. The parallel execution will succeed when three actions succeed and will fail when three actions fail.

Next, the DoYourTrick Repeat decorator will execute its sequence forever. The sequence will execute FillYourTray
and will force FillTheShelf to succeed even if that subtree fails.

Then, the FillYourTray Sequence will move to the table, scan the table, raise the torso, and orient the head. It
will then repeat the following process forever. It will set the source tray to table and the side to RIGHT. It
will check whether the selected table tray is empty. It will invert the result of checking whether the back tray
is full. It will set the source tray to table and the destination tray to backtray. It will execute
RightArmFillYourTray and will force that operation to succeed.

Next, the FillTheShelf Sequence will obtain the REST pose, move to the shelves, scan the shelves, update the
octomap, raise the torso, and orient the head. It will then repeat the following process forever. It will set the
source tray to backtray and the side to LEFT. It will check whether the selected back tray is empty. It will
invert the result of checking whether the shelf is full. It will set the source tray to backtray and the
destination tray to shelf. It will execute LeftArmFillYourTray and will force that operation to succeed.

Next, the GoToTable Sequence will execute two MoveBase actions in order. It will first move to 1.5;-0.1;0.0 and
then move to 2.2;-0.1;0.0.

Next, the GoToShelves Sequence will execute two MoveBase actions in order. It will first move to 1.0;-0.3.0;0.0
and then move to 2.0;-0.3;0.4.

Next, the ScanTable RetryUntilSuccessful decorator will keep trying its sequence until it succeeds. The sequence
will raise the torso, orient the head, pause, start table scanning, pause again, clear the octomaps, retrieve
detected tomato cans, mark the table tray loci as occupied, and update the planning scene.

Next, the ScanShelves Sequence will lower the torso, orient the head, pause, scan the shelves, mark the shelf loci
as unoccupied, and update the planning scene.

Next, the ClearAllOctomaps ForceSuccess decorator will execute its sequence and will always return success. The
sequence will clear the left octomap and then clear the right octomap.

Next, the UpdateOctomap ForceSuccess decorator will execute its sequence and will always return success. The
sequence will clear all octomaps and will orient the head to 60.0 degrees pitch and 90 degrees yaw. It will then
orient the head to 60.0 degrees pitch and 135 degrees yaw.

Next, the UpdateOctomapLowerHalf ForceSuccess decorator will execute its sequence and will always return success.
The sequence will clear all octomaps and will orient the head to the specified lower and upper viewing angles at
yaw positions 90 and 135.

Next, the LeftArmFillYourTray Sequence will pick an occupied locus from the source tray with the left arm. It will
then try to pick an empty locus from the destination tray with the left arm. If that succeeds, it will try to
update the planning scene, pick and place the object, mark the source locus as unoccupied, and mark the
destination locus as occupied. If this operation fails, the ForceFailure decorator will release the destination
locus and will always return failure. After a successful destination-locus selection, it will release the
destination locus. If destination-locus selection fails, it will force a source-locus release to fail. Finally, it
will release the source locus.

Next, the RightArmFillYourTray Sequence will pick an occupied locus from the source tray with the right arm. It
will then try to pick an empty locus from the destination tray with the right arm. If that succeeds, it will try
to update the planning scene, pick and place the object, mark the source locus as unoccupied, and mark the
destination locus as occupied. If this operation fails, the ForceFailure decorator will release the destination
locus and will always return failure. After a successful destination-locus selection, it will release the
destination locus. If destination-locus selection fails, it will force a source-locus release to fail. Finally, it
will release the source locus.

Next, the TrayIsFull Inverter will execute its child sequence and reverse its result. The child sequence will try
to find an empty locus on the left side of the tray and will try the right side if the left side fails. If an
empty locus is found, the ForceSuccess decorator will release that locus and will return success. The inverter
will therefore report that the tray is full when no empty locus can be found.

Next, the TrayIsEmpty Fallback will first try to find an occupied locus on the selected side of the tray. If it
finds one, it will force the locus release to succeed and will then execute AlwaysSuccess. If the first branch
fails, it will execute AlwaysFailure. The fallback will stop when one child succeeds.

Next, the GetInUpTrayPose Sequence will obtain the arm joints for the UPTRAY pose and will execute both
arm-setting actions in parallel. The parallel node will succeed when two actions succeed and will fail when two
actions fail.

Next, the MoveTomatoCans Repeat decorator will execute its child four times. Each repetition will force a
KeepRunningUntilFailure decorator to succeed. The KeepRunningUntilFailure decorator will execute a Fallback that
first tries to fill the tray with the left arm and then the right arm. If that sequence fails, it will try the
right-arm operation alone. The fallback will stop when one operation succeeds. The loop will restart the operation
while it succeeds and will stop when it fails.

Next, the root-level Repeat and ForceSuccess decorators in the supporting routines will preserve their specified
repetition and success behavior. A Repeat decorator with num_cycles equal to -1 will continue indefinitely, while
a ForceSuccess decorator will return success regardless of its child result.

Parameters: GetArmJointsForPose will use pose REST or UPTRAY and will store the resulting joints under
{joints_pose}. TraySetLoci will use tray_name table or shelf, locus_poses {detected_tomato_cans} or
{tomato_can_stocking_target_poses}, and occupied true or false. TrayPickOccupiedLocus will use tray_name
{src_tray} or {tray}, side LEFT, RIGHT, or {side}, and will store the selected locus under {src_locus_id} or
{locus_id}. TrayPickEmptyLocus will use tray_name {dst_tray} or {tray}, side LEFT or RIGHT, and will store the
selected locus under {dst_locus_id} or {locus_id} and its pose under {dst_locus_pose} or {locus_pose}.
PickAndPlaceLeft will use object_id {src_locus_id}, target_pose {dst_locus_pose}, and flat_hand_mode true.
PickAndPlaceRight will use object_id {src_locus_id}, target_pose {dst_locus_pose}, and flat_hand_mode false.
TraySetLocusState will use tray_name {src_tray} or {dst_tray}, locus_id {src_locus_id} or {dst_locus_id}, and
occupied false or true.
