The mission describes checking whether the robot is full before moving to the closest resource and waiting for 15
frames.

First, the Sequence1 sequence will check whether isFull is true. If isFull is true, the robot will execute the
custom action goClosestR and then execute the custom action waitFrames in order. The sequence will stop if any
action fails. If isFull is false, the sequence will stop.

Parameters: The custom action waitFrames will use barout="", baz="", fooin="", and frames="15".
