The mission describes a sequence that announces a thought prompt, generates an answer, and announces the generated
answer.

First, the Sequence will execute SaySomething, ThinkWhatToSay, and SaySomething in order and will stop if any
action fails.

Next, the robot will use SaySomething to say "Start thinking...".

Then, it will use ThinkWhatToSay to generate text and store it under the_answer.

Finally, it will use SaySomething to say the value read from the_answer.
