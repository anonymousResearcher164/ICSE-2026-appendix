The mission describes a repeated navigation routine between two target poses.

First, the Repeat node will execute its child sequence 10 times.

Next, the sequence will execute the NavigateToAction with goal 0.5;0.5;0, followed by the NavigateToAction with
goal -0.5;-0.5;0, in order, and it will stop if either action fails.

Then, the robot will execute the NavigateToAction with goal 0.5;0.5;0.

Finally, it will execute the NavigateToAction with goal -0.5;-0.5;0.
