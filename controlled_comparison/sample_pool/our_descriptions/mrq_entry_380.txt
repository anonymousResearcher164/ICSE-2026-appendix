The mission describes checking whether the robot is full, moving to the closest resource, and waiting for 15
frames.

First, the root sequence will execute the following actions in order and stop if any action fails. The robot will
check whether isFull is true, execute custom action goClosestR, and execute custom action waitFrames with
barout="", baz="", fooin="", and frames=15.

Next, it will check whether isFull is true.

Then, it will execute custom action goClosestR.

Finally, it will execute custom action waitFrames with barout="", baz="", fooin="", and frames=15.
