The mission describes the generation of grasping candidates from a segmented tabletop scene, including optional
desired-object extraction and tabletop clustering.

First, the root Sequence will execute the RetryUntilSuccesful subtree, the ConditionalSequence subtree, and the
GenerateGraspingCandidates action in order and stop if any action fails.

Next, the RetryUntilSuccesful subtree will retry the TableTopSegmentation action until it succeeds or three
attempts are completed.

Next, the ConditionalSequence subtree will execute its actions in order while checking whether the condition False
is true for each action. If the condition False is false, the sequence will end. The DesiredObjectEntryCheck
decorator will check the desired_object entry before allowing the ObjectCloudExtraction action to execute. The
TableTopClustering action will execute after the decorated action succeeds.

Finally, the GenerateGraspingCandidates action will execute.

Parameters: The TableTopSegmentation action will receive original_cloud="{original_cloud}",
image_scene="{image_scene}", table_height="{table_height}",
original_cloud_transformed="{original_cloud_transformed}", tabletop_cloud="{tabletop_cloud}", and
plane_cloud="{plane_cloud}", and it will store nonplane_cloud under {nonplane_cloud}. The DesiredObjectEntryCheck
decorator will receive entry="{desired_object}". The ObjectCloudExtraction action will receive
original_cloud="{original_cloud}", image_scene="{image_scene}", object_cloud="{object_cloud}",
tabletop_cloud="{tabletop_cloud}", and desired_object="{desired_object}". The TableTopClustering action will
receive object_cloud="{object_cloud}" and tabletop_cloud="{tabletop_cloud}". The GenerateGraspingCandidates action
will receive object_cloud="{object_cloud}", table_height="{table_height}", grasp_candidates="{grasp_candidates}",
and original_cloud_transformed="{original_cloud_transformed}".
