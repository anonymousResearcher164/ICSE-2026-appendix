The mission describes fixing the rudder and then waiting for one second.

First, the root sequence will execute the following actions in order and stop if any action fails. The robot will
execute custom action FixRudder with depth 1.0, rudder 2.0, and speed_knots 3.0. It will delay execution for 1000
milliseconds before running the AlwaysSuccess action.

Next, it will execute AlwaysSuccess after the delay.
