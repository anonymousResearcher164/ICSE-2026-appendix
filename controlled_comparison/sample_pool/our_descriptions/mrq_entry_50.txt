The mission describes the robot fixing a rudder and then waiting for one second.

First, the robot will execute the following actions in order and stop the sequence if any action fails. It will
execute custom action FixRudder. It will execute DelayFor and delay its child for 1000 milliseconds. It will then
execute custom action AlwaysSuccess.

Parameters: The custom action FixRudder will use depth 1.0, rudder 2.0, and speed_knots 3.0.
