The mission describes fixing a rudder and then waiting for a delayed successful completion.

First, the robot will execute the custom action FixRudder and the custom action DelayFor in order and stop the
sequence if either action fails.

Next, it will execute the custom action DelayFor after its delay timer expires. It will then execute the custom
action AlwaysSuccess.

Parameters: The custom action FixRudder will use depth="1.0", rudder="2.0", and speed_knots="3.0". The custom
action DelayFor will use delay_msec="1000".
