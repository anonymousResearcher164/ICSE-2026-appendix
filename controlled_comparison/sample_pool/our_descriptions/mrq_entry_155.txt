The mission describes clearing the global navigation cost map and initializing robot seeking.

First, the robot will execute the actions in sequence and stop if any action fails.

Next, it will execute the ROS action Nav2ClearGlobalCostMap.

Finally, it will execute the custom action RobotSeekInitAction.

Parameters: The custom action RobotSeekInitAction will use robot_seek_1.json as its game_file parameter.
