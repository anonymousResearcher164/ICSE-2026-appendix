The mission describes repeated speech transcription and speech synthesis.

First, the robot will execute the enclosed sequence three times.

Next, it will execute SpeechToTextActionClient, TextToSpeechActionClient, SpeechToTextActionClient, and
TextToSpeechActionClient in order and stop the sequence if any action fails.

Parameters: The first SpeechToTextActionClient will store its text under {said}. The first TextToSpeechActionClient
will use the value read from {said} as its msg. The second SpeechToTextActionClient will store its text under
{said}. The second TextToSpeechActionClient will use the value read from {said} as its msg.
