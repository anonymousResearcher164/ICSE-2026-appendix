The mission describes a robot repeating a speech interaction three times.

First, The robot will execute the sequence three times.

Next, it will execute the following actions in order and stop the sequence if any action fails. It will execute
SpeechToTextActionClient and store the recognized text under the said blackboard key. It will execute
TextToSpeechActionClient using the text stored under the said blackboard key. It will execute
SpeechToTextActionClient again and store the recognized text under the said blackboard key. It will execute
TextToSpeechActionClient again using the text stored under the said blackboard key.
